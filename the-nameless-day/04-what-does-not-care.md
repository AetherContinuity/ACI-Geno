## BANA, Instruction 4/IX

**Operational continuity on unnamed days**
*Adopted unanimously.*

**§ 1 Purpose.** This instruction ensures that BANA's operations continue on days not entered in the state calendar.

**§ 2 Scope.** The instruction applies to all greenhouses, stores, transport systems and administrative units.

**§ 3 Continuity of production.** The growth of plants shall not be interrupted for lack of a calendar entry. Ripe fruit shall be identified, and its use planned in advance.

**§ 4 Right of use.** The right to use a fruit shall be confirmed before use. If the right of use cannot be confirmed, the fruit shall be placed in abeyance pending a decision. If the fruit does not keep for the duration of the abeyance, a deviation report shall be filed.

**§ 5 Deviations.** If a fruit ripens on a day not in the state calendar, the responsible unit shall:

- record the observation of ripeness;
- notify BANA;
- await confirmation of the right of use;
- document any loss.

**§ 6 Authority.** This instruction confers no right to use a fruit without a separate decision.

**§ 7 Monitoring.** The functioning of the instruction shall be assessed annually. The first assessment shall be made after the next unnamed day.

**Annex 1. Exercise**

The exercise simulated a bunch ripening on an unnamed day in a greenhouse.

Staff identified the situation correctly. The observation of ripeness was recorded. Notification was sent to BANA. BANA received the notification. Confirmation of the right of use was awaited.

During the exercise the bunch did not rot. At the end of the exercise the bunch was still ripe.

The exercise was assessed as successful.

**Annex 2. Post-exercise review**

Staff were asked whether the exercise had corresponded to a real situation. The answer was yes.

Staff were asked whether the bunch could have been eaten during the exercise. The answer was no. Reason: no separate decision on the right of use had been issued in the exercise. No decision had been issued because the exercise contained no real bunch.

The review found that the instruction had functioned as planned.

**Closing entry.** Instruction 4/IX was filed. An appropriation was reserved for the next unnamed day. The appropriation was not used, because the day could not be confirmed as a budget period. It was carried forward to the following year. The following year it was carried forward again.
