## What happens in Version B

7.11.2034, 18:15. The date field on the Airlock 3 display goes blank. In Version B the access system fails open: *DATE INVALID. ACCESS GRANTED (DEFAULT).* No one has a right to enter. The door simply opens.

Akseli goes in and eats **one** banana: 142 → 141. He eats it alone. His father does not eat. The Association counts 141 and does **not** eat the rest. The remaining 141 rot overnight, as in Version A. One act creates a deviation in the books. It does not save the bunch.

## Five chapters, initial letters spelling **EATEN**

1. **E**leven Seconds: the eating and the notebook entry that took eleven seconds to write.
2. **A**udit: the Association counts 141. Strömberg arrives and finds that the books balance for the first time. K. and V. arrive and find nothing to assess.
3. **T**he Field With No Box: the State has no form field for an eaten banana. BANA creates the category EATEN.
4. **E**ighth Inquiry: the harbour master at Narvik is answered at last: *"There is no shipment."*
5. **N**eulamäki: the father, the window, the pencil mark, the sentence.

Chapter titles may change. The initial letters may not.

## Keep from your novel

- Akseli as the frost man, the thermometer who acts once.
- *"Heat is a moralist."* *"It is a state of verbs."* *"I was invited as a thermometer."* *"It tasted like a banana."*
- The father's line: *"I know the look of a plant that is being counted instead of picked."* He says it in Version B only.
- Ilmari, Aila, the blue notebook.
- The pencil mark *ammunition* on the window frame in Neulamäki. It already appears in Version A, seen by K. and not understood. In *Neulamäki* it must acquire a meaning of its own, not merely remind the reader that it was there before. How it opens is yours to decide.

## Change from your novel

- **Remove** the annex to the Association's rules granting harvest rights. No one has a right to eat. There is only a door that opened.
- **Calendar:** ten-day weeks, three per month. The Month of Reporting falls in October–November, not March.
- **Counts:** the bunch has 142 bananas. Not 11, not 14.
- **Remove** the banana plant rooting from a fallen leaf. The shoot appears at the root of Row 14 in the shared ending, not before.
- **Timeline:** use the dates in KANON.md, section 1.
- **Register:** cut confessional emphasis by about a third (*"I want that said, and I want it said loudly"*). Version A is dry. Version B may be warmer, but it must not explain the joke.
- **Do not narrate** Version A's investigation (Strömberg's solution, the acrostic, pages 41–44). Version B does not know about it.
