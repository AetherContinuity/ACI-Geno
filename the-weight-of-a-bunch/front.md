# The Weight of a Bunch

## A Novel

*Being a book of the wet season at Mustakorpi, and of one banana.*

Set at 61 degrees north, in a country that reports its harvest instead of picking it: a frost-maintenance man, an audit office that audits itself, a pledge worth 2.4 billion Banana Units, thirty citizen capuchins, a melting snowman with a carrot that has become a measurement, and the first banana anyone ever ate.

---

### A note on this book

This is an independent work of fiction. It is not a translation. It grew out of three Finnish-language self-published chronicles — *Bannanivaltio* (2026), *Puijon piiritys* (2028–2033) and *Puijon terttu* (BANA audit report 1/IX) — the way a shoot comes up at the root of a banana that has already died: same root, different plant. Its characters, plot and voice are new; what it borrows is the weather, the hill, the glass, and the arithmetic.

Numerical and institutional absurdities in this book are inventions of the satire. Any resemblance to actual steering groups is, as in the source tradition, regrettably unavoidable.
